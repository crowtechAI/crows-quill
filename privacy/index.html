Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

The Crow's Quill is a small illustration studio. We collect only what we need to quote, create and deliver your commission, take payment, and keep in touch. We don't sell your data, run advertising trackers, or use analytics cookies on this site.

1. Who we are

"The Crow's Quill", "we" and "us" means The Crow's Quill Studio, the artist and operator of this website. For anything in this policy, contact us at contact@crowsquill.art.

2. What we collect

InformationWhen and why
Name and email addressWhen you enquire or book. To identify you, send quotes, payment links and updates, and let you track your commission.
Book or series title and commission briefWhat you tell us about your characters, scenes and references, so we can create the artwork.
Files you uploadReference images or notes you attach to your commission. Please upload only material you have the right to share.
Typed signature and acceptance dateWhen you accept the Commission Agreement, as a record of the contract.
Quote, deposit and payment statusTo manage your commission and know when payments are complete. We do not see or store your card number; payments are handled by Stripe.
Messages and notesNotes you send through the client portal and our internal notes about your commission.
Technical dataStandard server and security logs (such as IP address and browser type) created by our hosting and database providers when you use the site, used to keep it working and secure. Our booking form also includes spam protection.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for: quoting, scheduling, creating and delivering your artwork.
  • To take and record payments and keep the financial records we are required to keep.
  • To email you about your commission, including quotes, contract, payment links, sketches and delivery.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To respond to your questions and meet our legal obligations.

We only send emails related to your commission. We don't send marketing email unless you ask us to.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, only to run the service:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseDatabase, file storage and studio login. Our project is hosted in the West EU (Ireland) region.
StripeSecure card payments for deposits and balances. Stripe has its own privacy policy and is responsible for your card details.
ResendSending commission emails such as payment links.
VercelHosting and delivering this website.
Google FontsLoads the site's fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, which can see your IP address.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights. We never sell your personal information.

5. International transfers

Some of these providers (for example Stripe, Resend, Vercel and Google) may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent frameworks.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The public pages don't set tracking cookies. The studio login (for the artist only) stores a sign-in session in the browser's local storage so it keeps you signed in. Stripe may set its own cookies on its checkout pages to process payment and prevent fraud.

7. How long we keep it

We keep commission details, contracts and payment records for as long as needed to deliver the work, handle any follow-up questions or disputes, and meet tax and accounting obligations. Uploaded reference files are kept while your commission is active and for a reasonable period afterwards. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see section 8), except where we must keep certain records by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the UK and EU GDPR, and US state laws such as California's), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and ask for your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it; and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email contact@crowsquill.art. We'll respond within a reasonable time, and within any legal deadline that applies to you.

9. Children

Our services are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls so that only the studio can view commission records. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted on the site.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@crowsquill.art. See also our Terms of Service.
