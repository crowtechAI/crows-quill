Author Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The usual questions, answered before you book.

How fast is turnaround time for a portrait?

For a solo portrait, initial sketches typically arrive within 3–5 business days, with final delivery usually following within 1–2 weeks.

  • Factors: Number of characters, background complexity, and review speed influence total delivery.
  • Agreement: Your estimated delivery date is documented clearly in your commission agreement.
  • Need it faster? You can select an expedited Rush Commission during booking for tight launch dates.
What if my book characters have specific lore requirements?

The online booking brief includes dedicated sections for your characters' canon:

  • Lore details: Age, skin tone, hair texture, eye colour, height, scars, tattoos, and wardrobe
  • Visual references: Direct file uploads and moodboard links (Pinterest, ArtStation)
  • Verification: All lore notes are cross-checked during the initial sketch round before painting begins
How does payment work?
  • 50% booking deposit: Secures your reserved week on the production schedule.
  • 50% final balance: Due upon final approval before downloading high-res 300 DPI deliverables.
  • Payment plans: Flexible milestone instalments can be arranged for larger multi-character projects.
  • Fair cancellation: If cancelled, artist retains payment for work completed; any remainder is refunded.
Do you accept NSFW or sensitive themes?

Yes — romantasy, spicy scenes, and dark fantasy are welcomed regular commissions.

  • Tiers: From +$35 for 1 character, from +$55 for 2 characters.
  • Comfort levels: Choose between Suggestive, Nudity, or Explicit directly on your booking brief.
  • Strict policy: All depicted characters must legally and visually be adults (18+).
How do revisions work?
  • Sketch phase: 2 complete rounds of consolidated changes (pose, anatomy, expressions, styling).
  • Final phase: Minor corrections to lighting, atmospheric effects, and colour balance.
  • Major overhauls: Completely new poses or concepts requested after sketch sign-off may incur an additional fee.
Who owns the artwork, and what files do I receive?
  • Your intellectual property: You retain 100% of all rights in your original characters, series, and books.
  • Artist copyright: Artist retains artwork copyright and agrees never to exploit your characters.
  • Deliverables: Full-resolution 300 DPI master files (PNG/JPEG) formatted for crisp print and web display.
Can I edit the artwork or use it with AI tools?
  • Allowed: You are free to crop, resize, and add title/marketing text overlays for promotional use.
  • Restricted: Repainting, redrawing, or recolouring the artwork requires written artist permission.
  • Zero AI Policy: Artwork may never be used to train generative AI models or uploaded to AI image generators.
Can you keep my art private until my cover reveal?

Yes. Simply note your cover reveal or book launch date on the booking form. The studio will strictly hold back public portfolio posting until you've unveiled the artwork to your audience.

What if I need it by a fixed date?

Delivery dates are estimates and depend on feedback speed. If you have a specific launch or preorder deadline, enter it in the calendar. For deadlines closer than our standard buffer, select the Rush Commission (+30%) option to secure priority production.
