Author Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before initiating your commission slot.

How fast is the turnaround time for a portrait?
Solo portraits typically receive preliminary composition sketches within 3–5 business days. Once you approve the sketch, final painted rendering takes another 4–6 days. Most authors receive final delivery within 10–14 days total.
What if my book characters have specific lore requirements?
Our online brief form allows you to attach character physical notes (skin tone, eye colors, scars, tattoos, weapons) alongside Pinterest boards or moodboards. We carefully cross-reference your descriptions during the sketch phase.
How does payment work?
A 50% non-refundable deposit books your calendar slot. The remaining 50% is due only after you have reviewed and approved your final watermarked artwork, right before uncompressed delivery.
Do you accept NSFW or sensitive themes?
Yes! We cater to romantasy and dark romance authors. All depicted characters must strictly be consenting adults (18+). You can select spicy/NSFW options when filling out your brief.
