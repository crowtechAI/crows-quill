Rights & Usage

Licensing

What you can do with your artwork: promote your book for free, sell it on merchandise with the add-on licence, or agree publishing use separately.

Included With All Art

Author Promotional Licence

Standard author commissions include permission to use the finished artwork to promote your book, characters or series: social media, your website and newsletters, paid advertising, retailer graphics, character reveals, launch and preorder graphics, events and displays, banners, and free bookmarks, postcards, art cards, preorder gifts, reader swag and other free promotional items. The artwork may be cropped, resized and have text added where reasonably required for permitted promotional use.

+50% Add-on

Commercial Merchandise Licence

A flat commercial fee of +50% of the original artwork cost allows you to use the commissioned artwork on promotional items for sale, for example postcards, bookmarks and art prints. This licence does not include resale rights for the artwork itself, and copyright for the art remains with the artist.

Custom Quote

Publishing Licence

Using the art as part of the book itself is quoted separately: covers, dust jackets, endpapers, interior illustrations and special-edition components. Publishing rights and any required production specifications are agreed as part of the commission quote.

In Your Agreement

Using Your Artwork

Your usage licence begins once the commission has been paid for in full. Copyright in the finished artwork remains with the artist, and you keep all rights you hold in your own characters, books, series and world. The artist will not sell, license or otherwise commercially exploit artwork depicting your original characters without your permission, and may show the finished piece in their portfolio and on social media after delivery (or after any reveal date agreed with you).

You may crop, resize and add text where your licence allows, but you may not repaint, redraw, retouch, recolour or otherwise alter the artwork without the artist’s written permission, and it may not be used to train generative AI systems or be uploaded to generative AI services to create replacement or derivative artwork. Where credit is reasonably practical, the preferred credit is “Art by The Crow’s Quill”. Credit is appreciated when the artwork itself is being showcased, but isn’t required on every advertisement, graphic or item.

Author Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The usual questions, answered before you book.

How fast is the turnaround time for a portrait?
Timelines are a rough guide and can change depending on what your commission needs. For a solo portrait, sketches typically arrive within 3–5 business days and the finished piece usually follows within one to two weeks, but the number of characters, background detail, extras and how quickly feedback arrives all affect this. Your estimated completion date is set out in your agreement.
What if my book characters have specific lore requirements?
The brief form asks for each character's age, physical appearance, hair, eyes, build, clothing and distinctive features, plus any reference links or files. Your descriptions get checked against the sketch.
How does payment work?
A 50% deposit books your slot. The other 50% is due before you receive the final high-resolution files. Payment plans can be arranged for larger commissions. If you cancel after work has started, the artist keeps a fair amount for the work already done and refunds the rest. If the artist can't finish, you're refunded for anything you paid for that wasn't delivered.
Do you accept NSFW or sensitive themes?
Yes. Romantasy and dark romance are a regular part of the work. NSFW art is an add-on: from +$35 for one character and from +$55 for two, with three or more characters quoted individually. Every character has to be an adult (18+). You pick suggestive, nudity or explicit on the booking form.
How do revisions work?
You get two rounds of changes at the sketch stage and minor corrections at the final stage. One round means one consolidated set of requested changes. Major changes after the sketch is approved, such as a new pose, composition, outfit or setting, may cost extra.
Who owns the artwork, and what files do I receive?
Copyright in the finished artwork stays with the artist, and you keep all rights in your own characters, books and series. You receive the finished artwork in high resolution, plus any additional versions agreed as part of your commission. Sketches, layered files, working files and source files aren’t included unless separately agreed. Your usage licence begins once the commission is paid in full.
Can I edit the artwork or use it with AI tools?
You can crop, resize and add text where your licence allows. Repainting, redrawing, retouching or recolouring the artwork needs the artist’s written permission. The artwork can’t be used to train generative AI systems or uploaded to generative AI services to create replacement or derivative artwork.
Can you keep my art private until my cover reveal?
Yes. Tell us your reveal date on the booking form and the artwork won't be shared publicly before it. If no date is agreed, the artist may share the finished piece in the portfolio after it's been delivered to you.
What if I need it by a fixed date?
Delivery dates are estimates and can move if feedback or information arrives late. If you have a fixed deadline, put it on the booking form so it can be agreed before work begins. A rush commission is an add-on (25–40% of the original quote).
